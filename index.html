About BCST

Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy

A subtle, gentle and non-invasive form of hands-on therapy that creates space for your body and nervous system to slow, settle and attune to its inherent health.

The practitioner listens through gentle touch and supports the conditions in which the body can find greater ease and health.

Sessions are an opportunity to step away from the busyness of everyday life and simply notice what is happening within you.

Craniosacral therapy was discovered by osteopath William Garner Sutherland at the end of the 19th Century. See more information about the history of BCST.

Gentle & non-invasive

You remain fully clothed and comfortably supported throughout your session. The work uses very light touch and periods of stillness.

Space to slow down

Modern life can leave us feeling constantly switched on. Your session offers time away from the noise to breathe, rest, settle and heal.

Listen to your body

There is nothing you need to achieve. You are invited to become curious about your experience and allow your own process to unfold naturally.

People may choose to receive craniosacral therapy during times of stress, discomfort, transition, tiredness or overwhelm, or simply as dedicated time for rest, recharge and wellbeing. Much dis-ease can be addressed with BCST due to its underlying support of the nervous system.

Please note: Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy is a complementary wellbeing practice and is not intended to diagnose, treat or cure medical conditions. If you have a medical concern, please consult an appropriate healthcare professional.

Your session

What to expect

Your 'cranio' session gives the body an opportunity to slow down, listen inwardly and simply be present.

01

Arrive & settle

We begin with a relaxed conversation about how you are feeling and what has brought you in. There is no need to have everything figured out beforehand.

02

The session

You remain fully clothed and comfortably supported on a treatment table (or chair if more suitable). Gentle touch and stillness allow you to become aware of your own experience.

03

Time to integrate

At the end of the session, there is time to integrate and notice how you feel.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing is ideal. You remain fully clothed throughout the session, so choose whatever helps you feel relaxed and at ease.

About me

Tina Mortensen - Diploma Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy

My journey with Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy began with sessions for my children in the early 2000s with a fantastic local therapist, Simon Gosling, then living in Motueka. My children were helped with birth trauma and discomfort or injuries from various tumbles. I also had treatments myself. My own tailbone discomfort which had plagued me since my teenage years was sorted within a few sessions. The 'magic' of cranio was intoxicating and transporting and, of course, so healing.

Fortune came my way a decade or more later and I had the opportunity to learn the art of cranio at Body Intelligence in Auckland over two years. I qualified with a Diploma in Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy in 2019. I feel privileged to have learnt the beautiful art of cranio, a profound, powerful yet gentle and wondrous therapy.

In 2019 I first started Attune Centre as a clinical practice for Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy. My clinic room is a calming environment in a tiny timber lined studio where you can step away from the demands of everyday life.

Everyone's body has the ability to self heal, it just needs the opportunity. Through the practitioner's focus on health and the deep listening to the body, acute and chronic states can be alleviated. A client is supported to be fully present in their body and the practitioner assists to help the client resource their system and strengthen inherent health.

My hope is that you leave your session feeling that you have touched a depth of stillness where self-regulation and health is found and have had time to pause, breathe and listen to yourself.
