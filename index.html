About BCST

Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy

Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy is a highly skilled method of listening to the body's inherent health expressions and a gentle form of hands-on therapy.

Through neutral touch and deep listening, the practitioner supports the conditions in which the body and nervous system can slow, settle and reconnect with a sense of ease.

A session offers time away from the busyness of everyday life — a space to pause, rest and notice what is happening within you.

Learn more about biodynamic craniosacral therapy →

Please note: Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy is a complementary wellbeing practice and is not intended to diagnose, treat or cure medical conditions. If you have a medical concern, please consult an appropriate healthcare professional.

About Me

Tina Mortensen

Diploma in Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy

My journey with Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy began in the early 2000s, when my children and I received sessions from a skilled local therapist, Simon Gosling, then living in Motueka.

My children experienced benefits following birth trauma and various injuries, and I also received treatments myself including for my own longstanding tailbone discomfort which improved within a few sessions. I was captivated by the depth and gentleness of the work.

In 2017 I had the opportunity to study at Body Intelligence in Auckland and qualified with a Diploma in Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy in 2019. Soon after I opened my clinical practice Attune Centre in a cute timber-lined studio on wheels.

I feel privileged to be able to offer this gentle and profound therapy, and to provide a space where you can pause, breathe, listen and reconnect with yourself.
